About Decredit

Who we are

Decredit is an independent insurance intermediary specializing in political risk insurance and structured credit for long-tenor, cross-border transactions. What we do

Services

Political Risk Insurance (PRI)

Protection for assets, contracts and cash flow against government action and contract breach in the markets where you operate or lend.

  • Expropriation and confiscation of assets
  • Currency inconvertibility and transfer restriction
  • Contract frustration or non-payment by a government entity
  • Political violence, war and civil unrest

Structured Credit

Insurance built around the actual structure of the financing — for banks, lenders and exporters looking to reduce credit exposure on long-term, cross-border transactions.

  • Protection for credit portfolios and project finance
  • Improved financing terms and capital treatment with regulators
  • Built around your deal structure, not sold off a shelf
